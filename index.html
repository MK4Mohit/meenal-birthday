For Meenal, On This Quiet Night

Somewhere between the stars and silence,
tonight gently learned your name — Meenal.

This is not just a birthday,
it is a pause in time,
where the universe softly admits
how rare you truly are.

Some people are moments.
Some are memories.

And then there are souls like you, Meenal —
felt before understood,
remembered even in silence.

May this year be gentle with you,
kind to your heart,
and filled with quiet magic
when no one else is watching.

Happy Birthday, Meenal ✨

— From Mohit
